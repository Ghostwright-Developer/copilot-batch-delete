# Privacy Policy

Copilot Chat Batch Delete Helper does not collect, store, sell, transmit, or share your personal data or your chats.

**What it does**
- Runs only on Microsoft Copilot pages: copilot.microsoft.com, copilot.com, copilot.cloud.microsoft, and the /chat pages of m365.cloud.microsoft, microsoft365.com and office.com.
- Reads the visible chat list on those pages so it can add checkboxes, and clicks Copilot's own delete buttons for the chats you select.
- Never reads, copies or sends the contents of your conversations. Chat titles are read only inside your browser to tell chats apart.

**What it stores (on your device only)**
- Your speed mode, how many chats you have cleared (a counter), and whether you've dismissed the review reminder. This uses the browser's local extension storage and never leaves your device. Removing the extension deletes it.

**What it opens**
- On first install, and after updates that add features, it opens a page on ghostwrightweb.com. The address includes only the extension version (for example `?v=1.1.0&src=install`). No identifier is sent.
- Review, share and support links open only when you click them.

**What it does not use**
- No analytics, no telemetry, no tracking, no ads, no remote code, no cookies, no browsing history.

**Permissions**
- `scripting` starts the helper in Copilot tabs that were already open. `sidePanel` shows the control panel. `storage` keeps the preferences above. Site access is limited to the Copilot pages listed above.
